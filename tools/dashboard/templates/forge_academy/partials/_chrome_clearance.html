{# aca-ux-05 — keep docked dashboard chrome off academy content. The dashboard docks three fixed widgets that the Academy does not own: the IQE mini-bar across the bottom, the "CLI Prompt / BRIDGE" panel bottom-left, and the assistant FAB bottom-right. Measured at the live viewport on /academy/missions, they covered four mission cards, and body had padding-bottom: 0 — so content at the foot of the page could not be scrolled clear of them. On the mission runner the IQE bar sat over the primary "Understood -> Continue" control, which is the forward action of every watch step. Fixed defensively and ACADEMY-SCOPED: these widgets belong to the dashboard shell and other canvases share it, so a global body rule would reach well beyond this change. Padding the academy's own containers lets their content scroll clear without touching anyone else's layout. --fa-docked-chrome is sized from the measured stack: the IQE mini-bar is ~68px tall and the CLI panel ~42px, sitting ~24px off the bottom edge. 7.5rem (120px) clears both with room for the focus ring on a control that lands at the boundary. #}
