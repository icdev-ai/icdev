Auto-dispatch

Only a role/topic its reviewed role YAML declares auto-eligible, with NOVA trust trusted or above, can be put on auto. Turning it on is your decision (decl-wft-ace-auto-dispatch): give a reason, an expiry and a daily limit. Turning it off, or stopping all, takes effect at once. A grant suspends itself if trust falls.
