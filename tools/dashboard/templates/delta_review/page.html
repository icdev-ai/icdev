Delta Review

The delta is the reviewable unit

A force_* override records that a human overrode, never what changed. Each row below is one claim-anchored diff: what the draft said, what it says now, and — for every sentence — whether the revision actually resolved the finding that blocked it, or merely reworded it. Approving requires a stated rationale; the diff is append-only evidence in trust_deltas and the disposition settles the matching approval_items ask.
