Engineering standards

What ICDEV's engineering holds itself to: one entry per standard, with its owner (a role), its sources (by reference, never copied), its scope and its check-as-code (an IDP scorecard rule, or a named manual evidence step). The life of a standard is propose → review → adopt → deprecate. Anyone, or an agent, may propose a draft; proposing decides nothing. A steward who is not the author reviews it ({{ templates.review }}), signs it into force ({{ templates.adopt }}) and later retires it ({{ templates.deprecate }}): each is a person's declared step, with a reason, bound to the entry's digest. A standard without a check is a wish: adoption refuses it. Nothing advances on its own. Status is the replay of an append-only, hash-chained ledger. Seeds already enforced by machine were recorded by a labelled bootstrap identity, which is never a person's signature. The citation catalog (args/standards_registry.yaml) is a different file and is cited, not copied.

The CoE's other records: exemptions (each waives a standard's rule for a scope, and always expires) and architecture decision records (signed, versioned, linked to the standards and requirements they serve). Each repo is scored against the standards in force on the standards scorecards, with its delivery evidence and its conformance bundle for ICDEV[OS]. A new service starts conformant on a golden path, which declares the standards it satisfies; adopting a standard that applies to services puts every path behind until a new version declares it.

Twin

The register's governance twin judges the ledger: a broken chain, a standard in force whose check no longer resolves, a source changed since adoption, a seed only a bootstrap identity recorded, a draft waiting. Each finding carries its thread to the ledger row it rests on. Recorded observations and their replay are on the governance twins page.
