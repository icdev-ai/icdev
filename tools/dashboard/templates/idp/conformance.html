Standards scorecards and conformance bundles

Each repo is scored against the engineering standards that are in force and declared for it ({{ view.get('config', {}).get('path', 'args/coe_conformance.yaml') }}). A standard checked by a scorecard rule is scored by machine across every service the rule applies to, with the live exemptions applied; an expired exemption fails it. A standard checked by a manual evidence step shows needs evidence: a person reads it, and it is never passed. A standard that is not adopted is listed, not scored. Delivery evidence is the DORA four keys and the rework rate of AI-assisted work, each with its window, its sample size and the digests of its sources. A metric with no data is unverified, never zero. Each repo's results go to ICDEV[OS] as one signed icos-conformance/v1 bundle: anyone prepares it, and a person who did not prepare it publishes it ({{ template }}), bound to its digest. Decisions behind the standards are on the ADRs page; the component ladder is on the portal scorecard.

Conformance bundle

Preparing records the repo's bundle, as graded now, at its digest. It decides nothing. A person who did not prepare it then publishes it to ICDEV[OS] or refuses it, with a reason. Only a published bundle leaves.

Twin

The Developer Portal's governance twin observes the bundles: a repo with no published bundle is unverified, a published bundle that carries a failing standard, an expired exemption or an unverified metric is a finding, and so is one waiting too long for a person. Each finding carries its thread to the ledger row it rests on. Recorded observations and their replay are on the governance twins page.
