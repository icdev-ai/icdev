Community of Practice learning paths

FORGE Academy tracks, one per Community of Practice: engineering (AIDLC), AI and data, application, and cloud. Every mission teaches one of this platform's own patterns and cites the files to read ({{ view.get('config', {}).get('path', 'args/academy_cop_tracks.yaml') }}). Your answers are graded on the server against the declared key, which never reaches this page, and every attempt is kept on your own append-only, hash-chained record. From that record alone the platform derives standard xAPI statements, one per learner and path: completed when every mission of the path is passed, progressed (never read as complete) before then. A learner is a pseudonymous account, never a name or a mailbox. ICDEV[OS]'s CoP charters link these paths by id. The statements go to ICDEV[OS] as one {{ view.get('schema', {}).get('batch', 'icos-cop-academy/v1') }} batch: anyone prepares it, and a person who did not prepare it publishes it ({{ template }}), bound to its digest. The engineering standards these missions teach are on the standards register and the standards scorecards.

Twin

The Developer Portal's governance twin observes the tracks and the batches (cop.academy): nothing published is unverified (ICDEV[OS] reads every path as unverified), statements a published batch does not carry are a finding, and so is a batch waiting too long for a person or one too old for ICDEV[OS]. Each finding carries its thread to the ledger row it rests on. Recorded observations and their replay are on the governance twins page.
