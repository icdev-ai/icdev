Scorecard exemptions

An exemption takes a component out of a scorecard rule, for a scope: a rule on one component, every rule on one component (service), the rule an engineering standard's check names (standard), or a rule on every component of a repo. It links to the engineering standards it waives. Every exemption expires: a request with no expiry is refused, and an exemption past its expiry no longer exempts. It fails the scorecard and the CI check until a person renews or revokes it. Asking decides nothing. Granting ({{ templates.grant }}) and renewing ({{ templates.renew }}) are a person's declared steps, with a reason, bound to the exemption's digest. The requester never grants their own. Nothing renews by itself. Revoking is a stop: immediate, with a reason. See the effect on the scorecard and on the standards scorecards per repo.

Look ahead

Judge every exemption as of a later date, to see what will have expired by then. A look-ahead changes nothing.

Twin

The Developer Portal's governance twin judges the exemption log: an expired exemption, one about to expire, one the migration gave an expiry that a person has not yet reviewed, a broken chain. Each finding carries its thread to the log row it rests on. Recorded observations and their replay are on the governance twins page.
