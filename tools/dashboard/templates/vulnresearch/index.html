Security / Vulnerability discovery

AI-assisted vulnerability discovery, proven or dropped

Defensive research on authorised targets only. A target is a local source tree under an allowed root (args/vuln_research.yaml), registered at the digest of its files; it is scanned only after a person who did not register it authorises it ({{ templates.authorise }}), and anything else is refused. Discovery gathers candidates from static analysis, dependency audit, an agentic source review through Cortex governance (it only proposes) and generated fuzz harnesses. A candidate becomes a finding only with a proof of vulnerability that replays in the sandbox and fires where its benign control does not; every other candidate is dropped and counted. Proofs are canary-only and sealed: only their digest is shown or exported. A patch is a proposal whose proof now fails and whose tests pass, reviewed by a person ({{ templates.patch }}) and never applied here. Each target's results go to ICDEV[OS] as one icos-findings/v1 bundle, prepared by one person and published by another ({{ templates.publish }}). Decisions others proposed wait on Decisions.
