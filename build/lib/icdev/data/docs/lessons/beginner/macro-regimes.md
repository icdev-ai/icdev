# What is a "regime" and why should I care?

A **regime** is the overall economic + market environment you're investing
in. FathomDesk labels each week (day, really) with one of a handful of
regimes: `EXPANSION` / `GREEN`, `INFLATION`, `STAGFLATION`, `DEFLATION`,
`RED`, `CRISIS`, `YELLOW` (mixed).

Why should you care? Because the same stock behaves differently depending
on the regime. A tech-heavy portfolio that's a rocket ship in `GREEN`
becomes a liability in `STAGFLATION`. Knowing the regime is how you pick
the right tools for the job.

## The four basic regimes

**EXPANSION / GREEN** — economy is growing, rates are low-to-moderate,
risk-taking is rewarded. Tech, growth stocks, cyclicals (stuff that does
well when consumers spend) all tend to do well. Most of the 2010s.

**INFLATION** — prices are rising fast, the Fed is hiking rates. Growth
stocks get hammered (future cash flows discounted more). Value, energy,
commodities, financials often outperform. 2022 was this.

**STAGFLATION** — worst of both worlds. Growth is slowing AND prices are
rising. Equities struggle across the board. Gold, commodities, TIPS
(inflation-protected bonds), cash tend to be the only things that work.
1970s classic example.

**DEFLATION / CRISIS / RED** — prices falling, credit contracting, fear.
Long-duration Treasuries (TLT), cash, dividend aristocrats, consumer
staples. Most equities crash. 2008 and the early weeks of March 2020
were this.

## How FathomDesk detects regime

FathomDesk computes a **macro regime** from ~14 indicators (unemployment,
rate curves, credit spreads, breakeven inflation, money supply growth,
PMIs, etc.). The combination signals which regime we're in.

You can see it:
- On `/market` — big stat cards + macro composite detail
- On `/radar` — Systemic Risk & Opportunity Radar (SROR) score
- In every Reading — the regime badge at the top

## What to DO with regime info

**You don't need to time the market.** You DO need to avoid the
single-biggest wrong move: being all-in on growth stocks during
`STAGFLATION`, or all-in cash during `GREEN`.

Rough playbook:

| Regime | Lean into | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| `GREEN / EXPANSION` | Equities, especially growth + cyclicals | Too much cash, long bonds |
| `INFLATION` | Value, energy, commodities, financials | Long-duration growth, REITs |
| `STAGFLATION` | Gold, commodities, TIPS, cash | Everything else |
| `DEFLATION / RED` | Long Treasuries, cash, staples, utilities | Growth, high-beta, speculative |

You don't need to rebuild your portfolio when regime shifts — but you
DO need to tilt 10-30% of your allocation toward the regime-appropriate
names, and trim the ones that historically get hurt.

## What FathomDesk does with regime

- The **portfolio Reading** flags "regime mismatch" when your book's posture
  (defensive vs aggressive) conflicts with the current regime
- The `/today` digest prompts you when the regime shifts to risk-off
- The alert system lets you set a rule that fires on any regime change

## The honest hard truth

Regimes can stay "wrong" longer than you think. In 2022's inflation regime,
people who tilted to value early did great. People who tilted in late 2022
right before the regime flipped back toward disinflation got whipsawed.

Regime-aware investing is about **tilts, not bets**. You don't go 100%
into what the regime favors — you just shift 10-20% of the book in that
direction and stay diversified.
