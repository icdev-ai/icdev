What is Edge AI?

Edge AI means running machine learning directly on your tiny device — not in the cloud! SparkPilot uses TFLite Micro to run AI models that can detect anomalies, recognize voice commands, or classify sensor data — all on a chip with just a few KB of memory. The Edge AI Agent manages the full model lifecycle for you.
