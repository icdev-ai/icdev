How Self-Healing Works

Embedded devices can crash — it happens! When your device has a HardFault, stack overflow, or watchdog timeout, SparkPilot's Knowledge Agent automatically analyzes the crash dump using AI, identifies the root cause, and generates a patch. If the AI is confident, it auto-heals the device via OTA. Otherwise, you get the analysis to review.
