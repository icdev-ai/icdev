What is a Device Fleet?

A fleet is a collection of devices (real or simulated) that SparkPilot manages for you. Each device runs FreeRTOS and connects back to SparkPilot via MQTT. You can send firmware updates, monitor health, and manage them all from here. Start with a Simulator device — no hardware purchase needed!
