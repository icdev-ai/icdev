# AI Transparency: OMB M-25-21 and NIST AI 600-1

Federal agencies deploying AI are required to be transparent about their AI systems. Two frameworks drive this:

## OMB M-25-21 (April 2025)

Requires federal agencies to:
- Maintain an **AI inventory**: every AI system in the portfolio with owner, purpose, risk tier
- Produce **model cards** for high-impact AI systems
- Report inventory to OMB annually
- Designate a **Chief AI Officer (CAIO)**

## NIST AI 600-1 (2024)

The AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) requires:
- **GOVERN**: Policies, roles, accountability structures
- **MAP**: Risk identification and context documentation
- **MEASURE**: Quantitative risk assessment (fairness, confabulation, robustness)
- **MANAGE**: Risk response and monitoring

## Key artifacts

| Artifact | Framework | Required by |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| AI Inventory | OMB M-25-21 §5(a) | All federal agencies |
| Model Card | OMB M-25-21 §5(c) | High-impact systems |
| System Card | NIST AI 600-1 MAP | High-risk systems |
| Fairness Assessment | NIST AI 600-1 MEASURE | High-impact systems |
