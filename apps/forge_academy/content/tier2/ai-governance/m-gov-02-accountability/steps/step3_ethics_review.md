# Ethics Review Schedule

AI accountability doesn't stop at deployment. It requires periodic ethics reviews to ask: "Is this system still doing what we intended? Is it fair? Has it drifted?"

## Ethics review frequency

| Risk tier | Review frequency |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Low | Annual |
| Medium | Semi-annual |
| High | Quarterly + triggered on incidents |

## Your task

Design an ethics review checklist for one of your AI systems. Include: (1) fairness check — what demographic parity metric to measure, (2) confabulation check — how to detect and measure hallucinations, (3) drift check — what behavioral signals indicate the model has drifted from intended use. Write the checklist as a numbered list with specific, measurable criteria.
