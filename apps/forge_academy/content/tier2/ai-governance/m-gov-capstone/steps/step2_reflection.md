# Capstone Reflection: The Hard Questions

You've built a governance portfolio for a high-risk AI system that affects individuals' lives. Three reflection questions:

1. **The tension**: ARIA speeds up investigations (public benefit) but may introduce bias (individual harm). How would you structure the fairness assessment to detect and measure that tension? Be specific about what you'd measure.

2. **The appeals question**: OMB M-25-21 requires an appeals process for automated decisions. In a law enforcement context, who should be able to appeal, what is the process, and who has final authority? Write the 3-step appeals workflow.

3. **The shutdown question**: Under what conditions would you recommend the CAIO shut down ARIA? Write 3 specific, measurable trigger conditions — not vague ("poor performance") but precise ("confabulation rate exceeds 10% in a 7-day rolling window").

Write 250-350 words. Be specific. This reflection is part of your certification evidence.
