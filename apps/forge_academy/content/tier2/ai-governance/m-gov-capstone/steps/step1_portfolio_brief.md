# Governance Capstone: Full Portfolio

Your capstone: produce a complete AI governance portfolio for a fictional agency AI deployment.

## The scenario

A federal law enforcement agency is deploying "ARIA" (Automated Risk Intelligence Assistant) — an AI system that analyzes case files to recommend investigation priorities. It processes PII and affects individuals' lives.

## Required artifacts

1. **AI Inventory entry** — system name, owner, purpose, risk tier (this is HIGH risk), data classification
2. **Model card** — intended use, limitations, fairness considerations, performance metrics
3. **Oversight plan** — CAIO designation, shutdown authority, appeals process
4. **Ethics review schedule** — frequency, metrics, conditions that trigger unscheduled review
5. **7-pillar intake score** — score each pillar, identify the 2 lowest and their remediation actions

## Success criteria

All 5 artifacts submitted via API. Each references the same `system_id`. The oversight plan includes an appeals process. The ethics review is quarterly (HIGH risk tier).

## Your task

Complete all 5 artifacts. This is your governance portfolio for the FAP certification evidence.
