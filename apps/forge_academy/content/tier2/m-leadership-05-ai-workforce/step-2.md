# Map Your Workforce AI Readiness

A workforce AI readiness assessment does not require an HR system or a formal survey. It requires honest answers to four structured questions.

## The Four-Question Assessment

### 1. Current Skills Inventory
For each role category in your org, answer: Do staff currently use any AI tools? If yes, which ones and at what level of proficiency? If no, what is the barrier (policy, access, training, awareness)?

### 2. AI-Augmentable vs. AI-Native Roles
Some roles are best enhanced by AI — the human stays in the loop but works faster and with better information. Other roles shift more fundamentally toward AI system design and oversight.

**AI-Augmentable (human + AI):** Analyst producing reports, PM tracking schedule variance, ISSO reviewing STIG findings
**AI-Native shift:** System architect designing AI pipelines, data engineer building RAG systems, DevSecOps engineer operating AI infrastructure

### 3. Upskilling Path Per Category

Use the FORGE Academy L1–L5 competency framework as your benchmark:
- **L1–L2** (AI-Aware): 4–8 hours of guided instruction, no prerequisites
- **L3** (AI-Capable): 20–40 hours, includes hands-on configuration
- **L4–L5** (AI-Native): 80–200+ hours, includes technical lab work

### 4. Timeline and Investment Estimate

| Role Category | Target Level | Training Hours | Cost Estimate | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Analyst | L3 AI-Capable | 30 hrs | $1,500/person | 6 weeks |
| Program Manager | L2 AI-Aware | 8 hrs | $400/person | 2 weeks |
| Engineer | L4 AI-Native | 100 hrs | $5,000/person | 4 months |

**Your task:** Complete the four-question assessment for your organization. Fill in the table with your actual role categories and realistic estimates. Where you find the most resistance or the largest gap between current state and target — that is your workforce risk.
