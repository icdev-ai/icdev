# Earlier Detection — Your Reflection

The value of pattern detection is not just speed — it is lead time. The analyst who detects a signal 30 days before their competitor acts on it has a structural advantage, regardless of how they use it.

## The Core Question

For your domain, what pattern — if detected 30 days earlier than current methods allow — would change a material decision?

This is not hypothetical. Every analyst has one. The contract that slipped because a competitor won quietly. The budget signal that arrived too late to respond to. The market shift that was visible in the data but not surfaced until it was obvious.

## Reflection Prompt

Answer two questions:

1. **Name one pattern in your domain that AI could detect earlier than your current methods.**

   Describe it specifically: what is the signal, what data does it live in, and how early is "earlier"? (Days? Weeks? A month?)

2. **What decision would change if you had that signal earlier?**

   Be concrete. "We would respond to the solicitation" or "We would brief the program director" is better than "We would know sooner."

---

## Why This Reflection Matters

The answers to these two questions define the business case for your pattern detection investment. They also define the acceptance criterion: if the detector surfaces this specific signal within the expected lead time, it is working.

---

**Your task:** Write your two answers. Then ask: Is this pattern detectable with rule-based configuration, or does it require ML-based detection? Your answer determines your implementation path. If rule-based, you can configure it today. If ML-based, you need training data — identify who owns it.
