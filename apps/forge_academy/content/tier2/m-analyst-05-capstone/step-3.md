# Your Capstone Intelligence Brief

You have completed the Analyst Track. You have configured a competitive intelligence agent, a RAG pipeline, an anomaly detector, and a report generator. This step connects all four into one intelligence product that reflects your domain, your consumers, and your decision support requirements.

## The Capstone Intelligence Brief

A capstone brief is a concise description of your end-to-end intelligence capability: what it collects, what it detects, what it reports, and what it predicts — and who uses each output to make what decision.

## The Four-Stage Summary Format

Use this structure to write your brief:

**Collect:** "My intel agent monitors [sources] for [target domain], delivering [output format] on [frequency]."

**Detect:** "My anomaly detector watches [metric] against a [baseline period] baseline, alerting when [threshold condition], with a [sensitivity level] false positive tolerance."

**Report:** "My report generator drafts [report type] from [data sources] using [citation standard], reviewed by [named role] before distribution to [consumer]."

**Predict:** "My trend forecaster projects [what metric or domain] forward [how far], with key assumptions [stated here], supporting [decision type] by [consumer]."

---

## Reflection Prompt

Beyond the mechanics, answer two questions:

1. **Who uses this pipeline's output, and what operational or strategic decision does it drive?**
   Name the decision-maker and the decision explicitly.

2. **What changes for your organization if this pipeline operates for 12 months?**
   Think in terms of: speed of decision, analyst capacity freed, intelligence products that become possible, or competitive positioning.

---

**Your task:** Write your Capstone Intelligence Brief using the four-stage format, followed by your two reflection answers. This is the deliverable you bring to your program sponsor or agency leadership to justify investment in an AI-assisted intelligence capability.
