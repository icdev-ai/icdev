# Assess Objectives, Not Controls

You will learn why ICDEV[OS] resolves evidence per assessment objective, and the five states an objective can be in.

> Facts in this lesson are current as of November 2026. CMMC Level 2 rests on NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2: 110 requirements and 320 assessment objectives in SP 800-171A.

## One objective at a time

An assessor determines each **objective** of a requirement. ICDEV[OS]'s control backbone keeps the objectives from NIST's own published content, pinned, and resolves each one for each system separately. A requirement is met only when every one of its objectives is met, inherited or not applicable, and at least one is met or inherited.

## Five states

- **met**: the evidence shows it;
- **not met**: the evidence shows it is not;
- **inherited**: provided by someone else, said with whom;
- **not applicable**: with why;
- **unverified**: anything else. No statement, a stale one, a stated gap. It is never met for want of evidence.

## Where statements come from

For a customer system, statements come only from that system's own imported evidence reports. The platform's own register speaks for the platform's system (`icos`) and for no other. A report counts for a system only when it is bound to exactly that system and marked no higher than it.

## Your independence

The assessor's duty is separate from the ISSO and the system owner. ICDEV[OS] refuses to give one person both.
