# AI in Your Organization — Opportunities

AI capability is not science fiction and it is not a distant investment. Five categories of government and DoD AI use cases are delivering measurable value today — without requiring your organization to build anything from scratch.

## Five High-Value Use Cases

### 1. Document Analysis
AI reads and summarizes large document sets faster than any analyst team. Contracts, solicitations, technical reports, policy documents, legal filings — any high-volume reading task is a candidate.

**Plain language:** "I can have AI read 500 pages of procurement history and give me a 2-page summary in 10 minutes."

### 2. Decision Support
AI-assisted dashboards synthesize data from multiple sources and present structured options with supporting evidence. The human decides; AI eliminates the assembly work.

**Plain language:** "Instead of my team spending two days pulling data for a program review, an AI dashboard has it ready in real time."

### 3. Code Assistance
AI dramatically accelerates software development for technical teams. It writes first drafts, reviews code for errors, generates test cases, and explains unfamiliar systems.

**Plain language:** "Our development team ships features faster because AI writes the routine code and they focus on architecture and mission logic."

### 4. Process Automation
Repetitive, rule-governed tasks — data entry, form routing, report formatting, compliance checklists — are prime targets for AI-driven automation.

**Plain language:** "Our weekly compliance report used to take 12 hours. Now it takes 2, because AI handles the data assembly."

### 5. Intelligence Synthesis
AI connects data from multiple sources — award databases, news feeds, financial filings — and surfaces patterns that would take an analyst days to find manually.

**Plain language:** "I can monitor 50 competitors across 10 data sources without adding staff."
