# Your Decision Memo

A decision memo is not a report — it is a recommendation with supporting rationale. It should be readable in under 3 minutes and defensible in a program review.

## The Three Elements of a Good Decision Memo

### 1. Recommendation
One sentence. State the path (Build / Buy / Partner) and the specific solution or approach.

### 2. Top 3 Reasons
The three criteria from your matrix that most strongly support your recommendation. Do not list all six — prioritize the ones that are decisive for your context.

### 3. Top Risk and Mitigation
Every choice has a downside. Name the most significant risk of your recommended path and state one mitigation you would put in place.

---

## Sample Decision Memo Format

> **Recommendation:** Buy — procure [Vendor/Solution] under an existing vehicle (e.g., GSA Schedule, CIO-SP3).
>
> **Reason 1:** Time-to-value is critical — mission requires capability within 90 days, not 18 months.
>
> **Reason 2:** Our team does not have the ML engineering capacity to build and maintain a custom model.
>
> **Reason 3:** FedRAMP High authorization is already in place for the selected vendor.
>
> **Top risk:** Vendor lock-in limits future portability.
> **Mitigation:** Negotiate data export rights and API abstraction layer in the contract from day one.

**Your task:** Write your own decision memo for your most pressing AI need — four sentences maximum. This should be something you could hand to a program sponsor today. If you cannot write it in four sentences, you are not yet clear enough on the trade-offs to spend budget.
