# Build vs. Buy vs. Partner

Choosing how to acquire AI capability is one of the most consequential decisions a program manager or CTO makes. Get it wrong and you are locked in, exposed, or three years behind schedule.

## The Three Paths

### Buy (Commercial Product / SaaS)
Purchase a pre-built AI solution — AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Palantir AIP, etc. Fast to deploy, but you inherit the vendor's data model, security posture, and roadmap.

### Build (Custom Development)
Build on open-source foundations or from scratch. Full control over data, model behavior, and roadmap — but you own the talent, timeline, and technical debt.

### Partner (SI or Managed Service)
Contract a systems integrator or managed AI services firm to build and/or operate on your behalf. Combines some flexibility with reduced internal burden — but introduces contract risk and knowledge transfer gaps.

## Key Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Data residency** | Can your data leave the boundary? IL4/IL5/IL6 environments have hard constraints. |
| **Customization** | Does your use case require domain-specific behavior the vendor does not support? |
| **Vendor lock-in** | What is the exit cost and portability of your data and models? |
| **SLA / uptime** | What is the mission impact of a service outage? |
| **Build cost** | Engineering talent is scarce and expensive — factor true fully-loaded cost. |
| **Time-to-value** | Mission timelines do not wait. A 24-month build may have zero program value. |
