# Draft Your AI Transformation Roadmap

A 4-quarter roadmap covers the period from "starting" to "demonstrable capability" — long enough to show progress, short enough to stay credible.

## The 4-Quarter Structure

| Quarter | Theme | Typical Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1 — Foundation** | Governance, data, infrastructure, baseline assessment | AI use case registry, policy framework, infrastructure readiness assessment, first training cohort |
| **Q2 — Quick Wins** | First deployed AI capabilities with measurable impact | 1–3 production AI tools, ROI baseline measurements, workforce AI-Aware certification for first cohort |
| **Q3 — Augmentation** | AI-assisted workflows embedded in core operations | AI tools integrated into standard workflows, AI-Capable certification cohort, updated governance based on Q1–Q2 learnings |
| **Q4 — Transformation** | Structural change in how mission is delivered | At least one transformed process with documented before/after metrics, leadership briefing with results |

## How to Place Your Three Initiatives

Take the three initiatives you identified in Step 1 and map each to the appropriate quarter. Use these placement criteria:

- **Q1 placement:** No dependencies on new infrastructure or significant policy change; can start now
- **Q2 placement:** Requires Q1 governance or infrastructure work to be complete first
- **Q3–Q4 placement:** Requires workforce readiness, data maturity, or organizational change management not yet in place
