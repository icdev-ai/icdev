## The AI Briefing Summary Format

A board briefing on AI transformation works best as 5 structured bullets — one per dimension. Each bullet should be one to two sentences and must be specific enough that a non-technical audience can ask an informed question about it.

## The Five-Bullet Template

Use this structure:

1. **Phase + Initiative** — "In Q[X], we will [specific initiative]. This is a [Foundation/Quick Win/Augmentation/Transformation] phase investment."

2. **Expected Outcome** — "The expected outcome is [specific, measurable result] — [quantify if possible]."

3. **Timeline** — "We expect to reach [milestone] by [date], with [key dependency] resolved by [earlier date]."

4. **KPI** — "We will measure success by [specific leading indicator] at 90 days and [outcome metric] at 12 months."

5. **Risk / Mitigation** — "The primary risk is [named risk]. We mitigate this by [specific control or decision]."

---

## What Makes This a Strong Briefing

- Every bullet has a number, a date, or a named deliverable
- No bullet uses the word "leverage," "synergy," or "innovative"
- The risk bullet is honest — it names something real, not "adoption challenges"
- A peer executive who has not attended any of these sessions can read this in 90 seconds and understand your direction
