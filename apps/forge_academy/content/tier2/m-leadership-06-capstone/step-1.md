# Building Your AI Transformation Roadmap

A roadmap is not a project plan — it is a strategic narrative that shows where you are going, in what order, and why. The best AI roadmaps sequence investments so that each phase makes the next one cheaper, faster, and lower risk.

## The 5-Phase Modernization Model

| Phase | Focus | Typical Duration | What You're Building |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Foundation** | Data, infrastructure, policy, governance | Months 1–3 | The preconditions for everything else |
| **2. Quick Win** | High-value, low-risk automations | Months 3–6 | Proof points that build organizational confidence |
| **3. Augmentation** | AI-assisted workflows at scale | Months 6–12 | Productivity multipliers across the workforce |
| **4. Transformation** | AI-native processes, new business models | Year 2 | Structural change to how mission is delivered |
| **5. Leadership** | Industry/government-leading capability | Year 3+ | Competitive differentiation and external influence |

## How to Sequence Investments

Start in Foundation even if it feels slow. Organizations that skip Foundation and jump to Quick Win find themselves rebuilding governance after a public failure.

Within each phase, prioritize by: highest mission value × lowest implementation risk. That intersection is your first move.

## How to Bring the Team Along

Transformation fails when technology moves faster than culture. Use Quick Wins as change management tools — visible demonstrations that AI is helpful, safe, and accessible — not just a CIO initiative.

## FORGE IGNITE Integration

FORGE IGNITE is the idea-scoring engine built into the platform. Any initiative you identify can be registered as a FORGE IGNITE idea, scored against mission alignment and feasibility, and tracked through the pipeline to funded execution.
