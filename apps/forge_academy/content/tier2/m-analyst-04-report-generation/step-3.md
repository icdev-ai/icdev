# Your Intelligence Product

This step is about capacity, not just automation. The goal of AI-assisted report generation is not to eliminate analyst judgment — it is to redirect analyst time from assembly to analysis.

## The Capacity Calculation

Most recurring intelligence reports spend 60–80% of analyst time on data gathering and formatting, and 20–40% on actual analytical judgment. AI-assisted generation inverts that ratio.

If an analyst currently spends 8 hours producing a weekly report, and AI handles 80% of the data collection and drafting, the analyst reclaims approximately 6 hours per cycle — 24 hours per month — that can be redirected to deeper analysis, source development, or additional intelligence products.

## Reflection Prompt

Answer three questions:

1. **What is the one report you produce most frequently?**
   Describe it in one sentence: frequency, audience, purpose, and approximate page length.

2. **How much time does it take today — from data pull to distribution?**
   Break it down: data gathering, drafting, review, formatting, approval. Where does time go?

3. **What would 80% AI automation of that report mean for your team's capacity?**
   Be specific: hours reclaimed per cycle, additional products that become possible, or analytical depth that was previously unavailable.

---

## The Strategic Point

The most valuable outcome of AI-assisted report generation is not faster reports — it is the analytical capacity that gets freed up. The question "What would my team do with 24 more hours per month?" is a strategic question, not a technical one.

---

**Your task:** Write your three answers. Then identify the one analytical product your team has always wanted to produce but never had the capacity to. That is your 80% automation dividend.
