# Certification Evidence

Review what you have produced across the two DocGen missions:

1. **SSP** from mission m-docgen-01: evidence of ATO document generation capability
2. **AAR** from Step 2: evidence of post-exercise analysis capability

For the FORGE AI Practitioner (FAP) certificate you need:

- The Foundation certificate (Tier 1 + your role's Tier 2 track + the adaptive assessment)
- An AADC design score of 80 or higher
- 1 GameDay scenario completed

DocGen artifacts do not replace any of these, and they are not attached to your Academy profile automatically. They are supporting evidence you bring to a human reviewer.

## Reflect

For each DocGen artifact you have (or would have): what doc type is it, what classification marking does it carry, what system or exercise does it cover, and which FAP requirement would a reviewer read it alongside? Then press Continue.
