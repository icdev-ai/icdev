# DocGen Portfolio Artifacts

DocGen doesn't just produce documents — it produces portfolio artifacts. A portfolio artifact is a generated document that is:

1. **Linked** to your Academy learner profile via `cert_token`
2. **Timestamped** with a creation and approval date
3. **Classified** with a CUI marking appropriate to the IL level
4. **Verifiable** via the artifact ID and a public verification endpoint

Portfolio artifacts serve as evidence for the FORGE AI Practitioner certification (FAP). If your DocGen session produces a valid SSP, POAM, or AAR, it counts as ATO evidence for your FAP portfolio.

## Why this matters

Before DocGen, producing ATO artifacts required manual effort over days. DocGen generates a draft in minutes and the HITL review takes another hour. Your portfolio builds automatically as you do real work.

## Your task

Review the artifact you produced in the previous mission (`m-docgen-01`). Open it in the Academy portfolio view at `/forge-academy/my-certificates` and confirm it appears as evidence for your profile.
