# Your ROI Playbook

A strong ROI playbook is not a spreadsheet — it is a decision brief. It answers three questions a budget committee will always ask: What do we fund? When do we break even? How do we know it worked?

## The Three-Question Test

### 1. What is the one AI investment you would fund first?
Name the specific workflow, system, or capability. Do not say "AI generally." Be precise. A vague investment cannot be approved, monitored, or canceled.

### 2. What is the payback period, and what is the 3-year net benefit?
Use the model you built in Step 2. State the numbers. Acknowledge your largest assumption (the one that, if wrong, breaks the case).

### 3. What KPI proves success in 90 days?
Choose one leading indicator that signals early momentum — not final ROI, which may take 18 months to materialize.

**Strong 90-day KPIs look like:**
- Processing time per document reduced by X%
- Number of manual review hours eliminated per week
- Analyst reports produced per sprint (volume increase)
- Error rate in a specific output category

**Weak KPIs to avoid:** "Stakeholders are satisfied," "AI is adopted," "Team feels more productive."

---

## Reflection Prompt

Write three sentences:
1. The AI investment I would fund first is _______, with a ___-month payback.
2. The assumption that could invalidate this ROI is _______.
3. In 90 days, I would know this is working if I see _______.

---

**Your task:** Complete the three-sentence reflection above. This becomes the opening of any budget brief you deliver on AI investment. If you cannot complete sentence 3, your initiative is not ready to fund.
