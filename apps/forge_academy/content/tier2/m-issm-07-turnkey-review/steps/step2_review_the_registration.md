# Review a System Registration

You will learn how to review a system record so that what the owner signs is exactly what you reviewed.

> Facts in this lesson are current as of November 2026.

## Signed as reviewed

When you take the review step, the record's digest is recorded with it. When the system owner signs, the gate compares the record's digest now with the one you reviewed. If anyone changed the record in between, the signature is blocked: return it to yourself for review. This is the same "signed as reviewed" rule for system registration, CMMC affirmation and tenant onboarding.

## What you look for

- **Scope**: every component has its CMMC asset category; out-of-scope ones are justified; no CUI flow reaches an out-of-scope asset or a provider declared not to handle CUI.
- **Providers and CRM**: every inherited control is in the matrix and given to the provider that leaves it; the platform's rows are shared, never the platform's alone.
- **Duties**: the people named are real people of the organisation, and no static separation pair (AO and ISSO, assessor and ISSO, assessor and owner, AO and assessor) is one person.

## Your organisation only

You review only your own organisation's systems. A step bound to the system's org refuses an ISSM of any other org, and a system you may not see answers exactly as one that does not exist.
