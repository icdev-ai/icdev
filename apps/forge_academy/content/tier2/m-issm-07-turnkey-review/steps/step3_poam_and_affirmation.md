# Hold the POA&M to the Rule, Then Review the Affirmation

You will learn the POA&M rules ICDEV[OS] enforces and your part in an annual affirmation.

> Facts in this lesson are current as of November 2026. The rule is 32 CFR 170.21 as ICDEV[OS] pins it; check the eCFR for the text in force.

## The POA&M rules

- A requirement the rule excludes can never be on a POA&M: adding it is refused with the rule.
- A **Conditional** status needs a score of at least **88 of 110** (0.8 of the maximum).
- A POA&M must be **closed within 180 days**; past that, the conditional status lapses.

ICDEV[OS] keeps POA&M items in the RMF's one POA&M store, not in a second list.

## The affirmation

An ISSO freezes an assessment by its digest. That makes a `cmmc-affirmation` item. You review it: the frozen assessment must be the one the item names, and recomputing it from the evidence now must give the same result. The **senior affirming official** signs it, never you. Entering it in SPRS is the organisation's own act.

## Words that matter

An evidence gap is **unverified**, never "failed" and never "passed". Nothing ICDEV[OS] produces calls a system compliant, certified or authorised: an authorisation is recorded, never issued.
