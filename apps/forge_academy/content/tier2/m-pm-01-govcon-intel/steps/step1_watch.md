# GovCon Opportunity Intel — Watch It Run

Finding relevant government contracts on SAM.gov manually means checking 2,000+ new opportunities per day, reading each synopsis, and deciding whether to bid. Watch ICDEV's opportunity scanner do that in 90 seconds.

## What the agent just did

For the fictional firm **Apex Federal Solutions** (NAICS 541511, 541512 — IT services), the scanner:

1. **Queried** SAM.gov for all opportunities posted in the last 7 days matching NAICS 541511/541512
2. **Found** 847 opportunities
3. **Filtered** by agency relevance (DoD, DHS, USAF) → 203 remaining
4. **Scored** each by capability match against Apex's past performance database → top 12 highlighted
5. **Flagged** 3 opportunities as **high-fit**:
   - `W912BU-26-R-0041` — USACE IT Modernization, $15M IDIQ, due in 14 days
   - `FA8771-26-R-0022` — AFMC Software Dev Support, $8.5M, due in 21 days
   - `70RSAT26R00000014` — TSA Cybersecurity Operations, $22M, due in 9 days
6. **Generated** a one-page opportunity brief for each high-fit opportunity

## The competitive advantage

Your BD team finds the same opportunities — 3–5 days later, after they get around to checking SAM.gov. With ICDEV scanning daily, you get 72+ hours of extra pursuit time on every opportunity. That's the difference between a competitive proposal and a rushed one.
