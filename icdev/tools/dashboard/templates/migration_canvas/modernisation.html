Migration Canvas / Modernisation

Modernisation

One legacy application, as ICDEV[IT] analysed it: the waves its own dependencies give, the CAM refactor and cloud-native jobs that ran over its source, and the icos-apptwin/v1 bundle ICDEV[OS] would twin. Everything here is read from what the tools recorded; nothing is run or planned from this page. Generated code is unreviewed until a person approves a job's output (a declared workflow step with a reason). The strategy and the portfolio decisions are ICDEV[OS]'s: record the strategy ICDEV proposes on 7R Assessment, and check a signed ICDEV[OS] export below before planning anything.

Waves

Derived by app_wave_planner in strangler order: leaves first, a dependency cycle moved together, each wave depending only on earlier ones; each wave carries its backout. A wave's estimate is the sum of its migration tasks' own estimated hours (none, never 0, where a task has none). Its dates come only from a team capacity declared in args/modernisation_capacity.yaml (hours a week, a start date, its source) that a person approved at its digest: one wave at a time, in dependency order, over Monday to Friday. Until then no wave is dated, and the page says why: no date is made up. Approve or reject the declared capacity below, with a reason: your step of decl-wft-mdz-capacity-review, bound to the entry's digest; a changed entry is reviewed again.

CAM refactor jobs

Each job ran a migrator through CAM and wrote generated files. Approve or reject a completed job's output, with a reason: your step of decl-wft-mdz-cam-refactor-approve, bound to the SHA-256 of each file. Approving applies nothing.

Features and processes

Derived without a model (bundle_features): features from the entry points the analyser read (static) and the routes an REA capture saw (observed; both when they agree), each with its evidence (file:line, route:, rea:) and capability hints from the reviewed rules in args/modernisation_signatures.yaml (a hint is for you to confirm in ICDEV[OS], never a mapping). A static process is the actions one controller offers, in source order, not an observed sequence; each step is normalised to verb:object, and the step signature's digest is what ICDEV[OS] compares across applications. Confirm or reject a process, with a reason: your step of decl-wft-mdz-process-review, bound to the signature digest you saw. A rejected process is left out of the bundle; a changed controller or rule set makes a review stale.

ICDEV[OS] bundle

What apptwin_export.py would assemble from this database, validated against the vendored contract (and the rules ICDEV[OS]'s importer adds to it). A null section is read by ICDEV[OS] as unverified. The 7R's confidence is the share of each strategy's criterion weight that was measured; an unmeasured criterion is left out of its score, never guessed. Waves derived before a plan are shown in ICDEV[OS] and never approved. The security domain comes only from args/modernisation_environments.yaml, as the deployment declares it.

Survivor IV&V

ICDEV[OS] endorses retiring an application into another only when the survivor carries an IV&V that counts. survivor_ivv.py runs ICDEV's own IV&V (ivv_assessor, IEEE 1012) over this application's modernisation outputs and records the result: each process area pass, fail or unverified (not assessed, manual, or semi-automated: never a pass), its findings, each requirement's evidence digest, the tool and its version, and the date, bound by one digest. A machine result is not independent: a person who did not build or approve this application's plan signs it off or refuses it, with a reason: your step of decl-wft-mdz-survivor-ivv-signoff, bound to the result's digest and taken once. The bundle carries the IV&V only while it is signed at the current digest; a newer run needs a new sign-off.
