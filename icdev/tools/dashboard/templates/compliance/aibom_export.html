Prepare and publish

Preparing records the AI-BOM, as built now, at its digest. It decides nothing. A person who did not prepare it then publishes it to ICDEV[OS] or refuses it, with a reason. Only a published AI-BOM leaves.

Twin

The AI-BOM twin observes the export ledger: an AI-BOM never published is unverified, never a pass; one published by its preparer, one waiting too long for a person and one published too long ago are findings. Each finding carries its thread to the ledger row it rests on. Recorded observations and their replay are on the governance twins page.
