Governance Twins

Twins that observe how a governed subsystem behaved: Cortex (TRUST gates, its audit table, findings awaiting a person, backends), ACE (stuck and failed runs, checkpoints waiting on a person, aborts, NOVA trust), the Co-Workers roster sources, and the feature-conformance audit. Every finding names its thread: open why to see the record it rests on. Replay re-judges the recorded evidence and says whether the verdict still comes out the same. A source that could not be read is unverified: it lowers coverage and is never a pass. Observing records evidence; it decides and changes nothing.
