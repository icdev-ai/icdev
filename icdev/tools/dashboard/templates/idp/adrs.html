Architecture decision records

Every architecture decision is a record: its context, decision and consequences, what it supersedes, and its links to the platform model's requirements and to the engineering standards. The flat log (docs/reference/adrs.md) is generated from the records and a drift fails CI. Anyone, or an agent, may propose an ADR; proposing decides nothing. A person who is not its author accepts or returns it ({{ templates.accept }}), and later supersedes or deprecates it ({{ templates.supersede }}), with a reason, bound to the record's digest. The decisions migrated from the flat log were recorded by a labelled bootstrap identity, which is never a person's signature.

Propose an ADR

A draft, accepted (or returned) by a person who is not you. Ids look like D-AREA-N. Requirements are platform-model requirement ids (e.g. PM-COE-08); standards are engineering standard ids (e.g. ENG-E2E-SPEC); every link must exist.

Twin

The Developer Portal's governance twin judges the ADRs: a flat log that drifted from its records, a ledger decision not yet exported to its record, a proposal waiting, a broken chain. A record the ledger has never seen is unverified, never passed. Each finding carries its thread.
