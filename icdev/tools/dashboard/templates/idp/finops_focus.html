FOCUS cost bundles

What each declared workload costs, read from a provider's FOCUS {{ view.get('config', {}).get('focus_version', '1.4') }} Cost and Usage export and handed to ICDEV[OS]'s Cloud CoP as one {{ view.get('schema', {}).get('id', 'icos-focus-cost/v1') }} bundle. Every row is validated against ICDEV[OS]'s pinned schema ({{ view.get('schema', {}).get('path', '') }}, schema digest {{ view.get('schema', {}).get('digest', '') }}, contract {{ view.get('schema', {}).get('contract', '') }}, a draft until ICDEV[OS] merges it); one row that fails refuses the whole export, named, so nothing is half read. A row belongs to the workload its Tags["{{ view.get('config', {}).get('tag_key', '') }}"] names; a row that names no declared workload is unattributed, shown, never dropped. Totals are the rows' effective and billed cost, to six places. Anyone prepares a bundle, which records it at its digest and decides nothing; a person who did not prepare it publishes it to ICDEV[OS] or refuses it, with a reason ({{ template }}). Only a published bundle leaves. Declared in {{ view.get('config', {}).get('path', 'args/finops_focus.yaml') }}.
