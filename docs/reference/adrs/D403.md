**Eleven dispatch guards and not one was a budget — so the cap refused the run's calls MID-RUN, and the aggregated verdict ships in `report` because the ledger it would enforce is a cost PROXY.** The scheduler ran six sequential early-return guards inside `_dispatch_to_claude` and five more in `_get_due_tasks`, each with its own log line and no shared record. All four USD guards the platform declares — `token_tracker.check_budget`, `module_budget_tracker.check_module_budget`, `cost_budget.apply_to_chain`, the proxy budgets — sit inside `tools/llm/router.py::invoke`, which a `claude_cli` dispatch never enters, so **every cap was bypassed at dispatch and then refused the work once the tokens were already being spent** (kpr-dup-09 was this shape: 95 `done`<->`backlog` flips in 5.5 hours, every individual transition legitimate). `tools/kanban/should_run.py::assess` folds all ten existing predicates plus the budget rung through ONE pure `classify`, calling each and re-implementing none (AST-pinned: no SQL naming the board table, the admission rule imported, the three budget seams called on the live path), and the circuit breaker's two inline copies of `failure_count >= max_retries` now read one function. **The survey is why `enforce` is off.** Replayed over 7,072 recorded scheduler dispatches through the SHIPPED `classify`: 71.89% `proceed`, **28.11% `wait`** — 0.00% in June and July, 63.00% in August, **100.00% in September** and 1,621 of 1,621 over the 30-day window. And **every fire is the module arm's TOKEN cap; not one is USD**: `module_budget_periods.spent_usd` is 0.0 in every month and `ai_telemetry`'s 13,995 rows sum to $0.00, because every call routes to a $0/1k provider. Corroborated from the writer side — 567 `budget exceeded` refusals in four hours across eight modules in the live NDJSON, 31,608 more in the rotated file, which are exactly the mid-run refusals. So the number says two things pointing at different repairs: the gate was genuinely missing since 2026-08-07, **and** arming it today would park every task on the board until 2026-10-01 — correct against the ledger and a stall against the estate. The repair is the token cap moved after routing or windowed to guard runaways, then a re-survey; **raising `monthly_tokens` to quieten the rung is forbidden**, because this rule is right about the ledger and the ledger is wrong about the cost. Capacity, sibling overlap and the external-repo park are deliberately NOT folded — they are decisions over the candidate SET or facts about where a task builds, not verdicts about one task.
