**The spike was right, the FACT moved, and the record shows both.** `twx-spk-01` ruled LocalStack NO-GO for air-gapped/classified because its 2026 single authenticated image validates an **auth token at container start**, and priced commercial CI per seat. That reading was correct and both facts still hold — of LocalStack. floci is a documented drop-in (keeps `/_localstack/health`, translates `LOCALSTACK_*` by default, consumes the identical stock `hashicorp/aws` provider shape) that validates **no token** and carries **no subscription**, so the spike's *conclusion* no longer follows while its *reasoning* stands untouched. The spike is therefore **appended to, not edited**: a rewritten spike destroys the reasoning that made the original call defensible, and a reader who cannot see the superseded verdict cannot tell a decision that was REVERSED from one that was NEVER MADE. **D382's LocalStack half is superseded on the licensing question ONLY** — its Batfish half (`twx-spk-02`) is untouched, and every non-licensing LocalStack finding STANDS: the Docker footprint still conflicts with this repo's pure-Python/offline preference (§2), Cloud Pods stays DEFER (our fixtures are already pure-Python and floci ships no equivalent), chaos injection stays NO-GO, and the **IAM-sandbox NO-GO and the performance-claim guard carry forward UNCHANGED** (D401). The spike's own follow-up `twx-ls-01` — an opt-in CI job requiring human sign-off on a paid subscription — is superseded by `flx-ci-01`, which builds that job with no subscription to sign off.
